Life Planner

How to use it

One page for the day: the one thing that matters, focused time to do it, and simple checklists for everything else.

1
No sign-up

Nothing to create or install. It works on your phone, tablet or computer.

2
Open the link

Tap the gold icon above. Your own planner opens, ready to use.

3
Start writing

Everything saves as you type, right in your browser.

##Focus: one thing at a time

  • Name your one thing. The big line at the top. Every focus block today should move it forward.
  • Block your day. Tap + Add block, set a start time and a length (an hour or two works best), and write the single task for it.
  • Follow the Now card. It shows the block you’re in, the minutes left and what’s next. Stay on that one task until the bar fills.
  • No plan yet? Tap Start a 90-min block now. Tomorrow, Copy yesterday’s blocks sets up your day in one tap.

##Lists, like a paper notepad

  • Type on any empty line and press Enter for the next one.
  • Tick the box when it’s done. Clear checked tidies the list.
  • Delete an item by emptying it and pressing Backspace, or tap ✕.
  • Make it yours: rename any list, tap the color square to change its highlighter, ⧉ copies it as a checklist you can paste anywhere, and + New list adds more.

##Today

  • Intention: one line on what would make today well spent.
  • Top three priorities. Three, never more.
  • Scratch notes for calls, ideas and reminders.

##Habits & goals

  • Habits: add one, then tap a day’s box when you do it. The week score fills in.
  • Goals: one card per goal for the month. Slide the bar as you go.

##Make it beautiful

  • Drop in a photo, or a .zip full of them, anywhere on the page. It becomes your banner, and the colors of the whole planner shift to match it.
  • Backdrop (button at the top) shows all your photos. Tap one to switch, or turn on Rotate photos daily.
  • Theme switches between dark and light.

##Your shortcuts

  • The round icons in the top right open the sites you use most: e-mail, calendar, Spotify, YouTube and more.
  • Tap the gear to open Admin. Add your own links, rename them, reorder them, or pick a new icon.
  • Icon style: Metallic matches your photo’s colors. App colors uses each app’s own colors, like Spotify green and YouTube red.

##A simple daily rhythm

Morning
Write your intention and three priorities. Block two focus sessions for your one thing.
All day
Follow the Now card. When something else comes up, drop it on a list and get back to the block.
Evening
Tick off habits, clear checked items, and nudge your goals forward.
✦

Private to you. Your planner saves in your own browser, so no one else can see it, including the person who sent you this. To use it on another device, open Admin, tap Export my planner, then Import a backup on the other device. Tip: add it to your home screen for one-tap access.
